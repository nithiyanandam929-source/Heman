Terms & Conditions

These terms explain how bookings, cancellations and appointments work at LE NOIR. Ticking the consent box on the booking page confirms you've read and accepted them.

Last updated: September 2026

This page sets out the terms on which LE NOIR Salon & Grooming House ("LE NOIR", "we", "us") accepts and carries out appointments booked through this website. By submitting a booking, you ("the client", "you") agree to the terms below.

1.Booking & confirmation

A booking is confirmed once you receive a booking reference on screen through this website. We reserve a chair and a stylist's time exclusively for you at your chosen slot, so please book only the services and times you intend to keep.

  • Bookings can be made for any date and time within our published business hours.
  • One booking may include a single service or a package; to book multiple separate services in one visit, please note this in the booking form.
  • We may call or message the phone number provided to confirm details before your appointment.

2.Arrival & late arrival

Please arrive five to ten minutes before your slot to allow for a brief consultation. A chair is held for fifteen minutes past the booked time; beyond that, we may need to shorten the service, move you to the next available slot, or offer the chair to another client, depending on the day's schedule.

3.Rescheduling & cancellation

Plans change, and we'd rather know than have an empty chair. You're welcome to reschedule or cancel by contacting us directly using the phone number in the footer of this site.

  • We ask for at least four hours' notice where possible, so the slot can be offered to someone else.
  • Repeated no-shows without notice may result in future bookings requiring a phone confirmation before they're held.
  • No advance payment is collected at booking, so there is no cancellation fee to worry about.

4.Pricing & payment

Prices shown on the services page are current at the time of booking and are payable at the chair once your service is complete. We accept cash, card and UPI. Prices may be revised from time to time; the price confirmed to you at booking is the price honoured for that visit.

5.Skin, scalp & health disclosures

Please tell your stylist before your service begins about any allergies, skin sensitivities, recent chemical treatments, or medical conditions that might affect a facial, shave or scalp treatment. We rely on this information to choose safe products and techniques, and we are not able to reverse a reaction caused by information that wasn't shared with us in advance.

6.Age policy

Clients under sixteen must be accompanied by a parent or guardian for the duration of the appointment. Some grooming services, including straight-razor shaves, are offered to clients aged sixteen and above only.

7.Personal belongings

We take reasonable care of the space, but LE NOIR is not responsible for personal belongings, including phones, wallets and jewellery, left unattended in the salon.

8.Conduct in the salon

LE NOIR is a shared, quiet space. We ask clients to be respectful of staff and other clients; we reserve the right to end an appointment early, without charge, if conduct becomes abusive or unsafe.

9.Photography

We occasionally photograph finished styles for our portfolio and social pages. We'll always ask before photographing a client directly, and you're free to decline without it affecting your service.

10.Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time as our services or policies change. The version shown on this page at the time of your booking is the version that applies to that appointment.

11.Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India, and any dispute arising from a booking made through this website falls under the jurisdiction of the courts at Chengalpattu, Tamil Nadu.

12.Contact

Questions about a booking or these terms can be sent to hello@lenoirsalon.in or by phone at +91 95660 31143.
